Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Data Collection

Dientempo does not collect any data from users. The app operates entirely offline on your device.

No data is stored, transmitted, or shared with any third party. The app uses only local system resources for speech synthesis and user preferences (voice selection).

Dientempo does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Dientempo does not collect any personal information from any users, including children under the age of 13.
