Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change the Spanish voice?

Tap the "Voz" link on the main screen (visible when not counting). You can select from any installed Spanish voice. To add higher-quality voices, go to iOS Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > Spanish.

Can I skip numbers or go back?

Yes! Swipe left to go forward, or swipe right to go back. Swipe multiple times quickly to jump faster (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 16 numbers at a time).

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Dientempo works completely offline. No internet connection is required for any feature.

Why does the app need Bluetooth permission?

Dientempo plays audio through your speaker or connected headphones. The Bluetooth permission allows it to route audio to AirPods or other Bluetooth devices.

How do I pause counting?

Tap anywhere on the screen to pause. Tap again to resume. The count picks up exactly where you left off.

Privacy

Dientempo does not collect any data. All processing happens on your device. No internet connection is required.
