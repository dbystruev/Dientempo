Dientempo is a simple iOS app that helps you learn Spanish numbers by counting aloud while you brush your teeth.

The app counts from 0 to 200 in Spanish, showing each number as digits and words while speaking it aloud. The default session lasts about 3 minutes — the recommended brushing time.

Features:
- Clear Spanish pronunciation using high-quality system voices
- Large, easy-to-read display with digits and Spanish words
- Tap to pause/resume, swipe to skip forward or backward
- Accelerating swipe for quick navigation
- Automatic dark and light mode support
- Voice selection for installed Spanish voices
- Works completely offline — no internet required
- Precise timing: each word finishes before the next begins

The name Dientempo comes from Spanish "diente" (tooth) and "tiempo" (time) — tooth time.

Whether you are a beginner learning your first Spanish numbers or brushing up on pronunciation, Dientempo makes it easy to practice every day without extra effort.
